// About the Fund

One Mandate.
Crypto & Precious Metals.

We are a private fund with two investment sleeves: digital assets (crypto) and precious metals (copper and silver focus). We deploy capital with discipline, research, and long-term conviction. For qualified and accredited investors only.

Two capabilities under one roof:

The fund — 1a crypto, 1b precious metals (copper, silver). Portfolio construction, risk management, governance, and reporting.
The fintech — we work with startups, building websites and systems that support founders and the broader ecosystem.

Together: a crypto- and metals-led fund and a fintech, XRP-oriented and sustainability-aware.

Fund overview
  • Strategy: 1a — diversified digital assets (XRP-led, core and infrastructure). 1b — precious metals with a focus on copper and silver.
  • Horizon & risk: Multi‑year, research‑driven mandate with the expectation of volatility; we do not chase short‑term trades.
  • Minimum: $1,100 for accredited and qualified investors.
  • Fees & alignment: Management fee of 1–2% of committed or invested capital, plus 20% performance fee on profits above an 8% preferred return to LPs.
  • Reporting & transparency: Quarterly letters, portfolio commentary, and an investor portal so LPs always know where they stand.
  • Governance & custody: Institutional‑style controls, segregated wallets and custody partners, and no commingling of fund assets with operating capital.

Founded and led by Mayieka — hands-on crypto experience and years building and supporting real businesses and systems. Most LPs hold 43 Industries alongside listed shares, fixed income, real estate, and cash — we manage the digital-asset sleeve of a diversified portfolio.

We run a fund for investors who want exposure to crypto and precious metals (copper, silver) with a dedicated, disciplined partner — not a side bet.

We design and build what early-stage companies need to launch and operate: websites, internal systems, and infrastructure that keep costs low and scale with growth.
